# FPVLovers Short Visual Identity

- Canvas: 1080x1920 vertical, 45 seconds, 30 fps.
- Base: cockpit black `#050810` with charcoal panels.
- Primary signal: orange `#FF5C00`.
- Telemetry: cyan `#00F2FF`; confirmation: green `#00FF66`.
- Typography: system sans, uppercase display, monospace telemetry labels.
- Motion: controlled signal sweeps, grid movement, fast panel reveals; no influencer-style bounce or fake urgency.
- Safe area: primary copy remains inside 90px horizontal and 180px vertical margins.

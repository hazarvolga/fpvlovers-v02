# MVP QA Contract

- Output must be 1080x1920, 30 fps, 45 seconds.
- Narration and on-screen claims must remain inside the manifest fact boundary.
- The comparison must state that it is specification-based, not hands-on testing.
- Upload payload must remain private and mark synthetic media.
- No third-party product imagery or unlicensed music is used in this MVP.
- Run HyperFrames `lint`, `inspect`, and `render` when the CLI is available.

# Affiliate Editorial Governance Design

Date: 2026-06-19
Status: Approved
Owner: FPVLovers
Product Review Editor: Hazar Volga Ekiz

## Objective

Make FPVLovers credible for affiliate applications without misrepresenting automated production, partnerships, traffic, or product testing. Preserve autonomous publishing for non-review education while requiring a real human decision for every product review.

## Publication Classes

### Product review

A page is a product review when its primary promise is a verdict, score, recommendation, or first-hand evaluation of a named product. It must remain unpublished until all of these exist:

- editor approval by Hazar Volga Ekiz;
- review timestamp;
- testing method: `hands-on`, `loaned-unit`, `supplied-unit`, `purchased-unit`, or `spec-analysis`;
- product relationship and compensation disclosure;
- evidence/source list;
- claims that match the recorded method;
- nearby affiliate/material-connection disclosure when relevant.

`spec-analysis` may be published as an analysis or buying assessment, but must not claim hands-on experience and must not expose an evidence-free numeric review score.

### Autonomous content

Tutorials, explainers, news, comparisons, buyer guides, reference pages, and community education may publish autonomously after deterministic gates pass. Required gates are source sufficiency, duplicate detection, metadata completeness, commercial-language review, affiliate disclosure injection, unsupported-claim detection, and route/link validation.

Comparisons and buyer guides are commercial but are not product reviews unless they claim first-hand testing or assign review verdicts based on testing.

## Product Evaluation Terms

FPVLovers may receive products as gifts or temporary loans. Receipt does not guarantee coverage, publication date, positive treatment, a backlink, or publication. FPVLovers retains editorial control. Every published evaluation records whether the product was purchased, supplied, or loaned, and whether compensation exists.

## Public Trust Model

- The About and Editorial Policy pages explain that routine educational content can be produced through an autonomous research workflow with deterministic safeguards.
- Product reviews identify Hazar Volga Ekiz only after actual approval.
- Article pages expose author/editor, last review date, methodology, sources, and commercial relationship where available.
- Affiliate applications use evidence-backed statements only. Program names may be listed as targets, never as active partners without confirmation.

## Affiliate Program Strategy

Apply in waves after the trust and content gates pass:

1. Broad networks and accessible direct programs: Amazon Associates, Banggood, AliExpress/Awin-or-current-network route, DJI/current partner route, BetaFPV, iFlight, RadioMaster, GEPRC, Caddx/Walksnail.
2. Specialist retailers through verified current application or direct outreach: GetFPV, RaceDayQuads, PyroDrone, Rotor Riot, NewBeeDrone, Unmanned Tech, Drone-FPV-Racer, HobbyRC.
3. Manufacturer and sponsor outreach after evidence of consistent reach and qualified engagement: HDZero, Flywoo, SpeedyBee, T-Motor, Foxeer, RushFPV, HQProp, Gemfan, CNHL, GNB, ISDT, ToolkitRC and similar FPV brands.

Program availability and terms change. Verify every application URL, geography, cookie window, payout method, restricted traffic source, and disclosure requirement immediately before applying.

## Success Gates

- No product review can bypass human approval.
- No public claim implies an unconfirmed affiliate or sponsor relationship.
- No score appears without the review evidence contract.
- At least ten substantial, recent, original commercial/editorial pages are application-ready.
- Legal/trust pages, article disclosures, internal links, metadata, schema, sitemap, robots, and CTA destinations pass automated and manual checks.

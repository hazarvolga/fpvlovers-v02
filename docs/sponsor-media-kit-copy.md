FPVLovers is a tactical FPV knowledge system helping beginners, builders, racers, and cinematic pilots make clearer setup, safety, and buying decisions.

FPVLovers works at the decision layer of FPV: where pilots compare ecosystems, check compatibility, learn safe setup, and decide what belongs in the next build. We are developing editorially safe collaborations for brands that can teach something useful, document a real tradeoff, or support the pilot community. Sponsorship is disclosed and never guarantees a positive conclusion.

## About section

FPVLovers is more than a stream of drone posts. It connects tutorials, engineering references, product context, racing intelligence, regulations, and practical tools into one English-language FPV knowledge system. The platform is designed for people entering FPV as well as builders, racers, and cinematic pilots who need clearer tradeoffs before they buy, solder, configure, or fly.

## Media kit introduction

FPV purchases are systems decisions. A radio changes the receiver path; a video ecosystem changes goggles, VTX, cameras, and upgrade options; a battery or charger decision carries real safety consequences. FPVLovers gives relevant brands a credible place to contribute to that decision process through transparent, useful, technically grounded formats.

## Independence and brand safety

FPVLovers does not sell guaranteed praise. Partners may correct factual product information and review confidential details when agreed, but they cannot remove material drawbacks, safety concerns, or competing alternatives. Paid relationships, supplied products, and affiliate links are disclosed close to the content. Claims must match evidence; AI-assisted research does not count as hands-on testing.

## Product seeding expectations

There is no universal traffic threshold that makes a brand send products. Niche relevance, average qualified views, geography, content quality, previous review evidence, reliability, and measured clicks/conversions often matter more than raw follower count. Use these as outreach planning ranges, not promises:

| Evidence level | Realistic ask |
|---|---|
| 1,000-3,000 qualified monthly sessions or 2,000-5,000 relevant combined followers with consistent engagement | discount codes, small accessories, giveaway support, or loan units |
| 5,000-15,000 qualified monthly sessions, 5,000-20,000 relevant followers, or roughly 2,000-10,000 average video views | lower/mid-cost supplied products and structured launch access |
| 20,000-50,000 qualified monthly sessions or repeatable five-figure video reach with conversion evidence | premium products, recurring seeding, and paid campaign discussions |

Do not wait for a magic number. Begin relationship-building with a clean media kit and one strong evidence-backed review; request a loan unit before asking for expensive hardware to keep the proposal credible.

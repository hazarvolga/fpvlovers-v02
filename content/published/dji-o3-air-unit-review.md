# DJI O3 Air Unit Review: The Cinematic HD Gold Standard

The DJI O3 Air Unit changed the FPV landscape by combining a high-performance digital video transmitter with a camera capable of capturing stunning 4K onboard video.

## 4K Recording and RockSteady Stabilization

By integrating DJI's proven RockSteady and HorizonSteady algorithms, the O3 camera eliminates the need for a secondary action camera on most 5-inch builds. The camera captures clean 4K/60fps video directly to its 20GB onboard storage.

## RF Performance and Penetration

Running DJI's O3+ transmission system, the link delivers up to 50Mbps bandwidth and a low latency of 28ms. Signal penetration in concrete structures and thick foliage is unmatched, providing pilots with absolute peace of mind during long-range excursions.

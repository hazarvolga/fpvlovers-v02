# RadioMaster Boxer ELRS Transmitter Review: High-Power Handheld King

We take the RadioMaster Boxer ELRS to the test bench and field. With its 1W internal transmitter module and perfect mid-size grip, it aims to dominate the radio scene.

## Ergonomics and RF Transmission

The RadioMaster Boxer has quickly established itself as a favorite among FPV pilots. It offers a mid-sized form factor that sits comfortably between the compact Zorro and the full-sized TX16S. Its standout feature is the full 1W internal ExpressLRS transmitter module, meaning you don't need external modules to achieve extreme range.

During bench tests, the RF power output remained exceptionally stable, showing minimal thermal throttling even after 30 minutes of continuous transmission at 1000mW. Gimbals are smooth V4.0 Hall sensors, which can be upgraded to AG01 CNC gimbals for ultimate precision.

# DJI Goggles 2 vs DJI Goggles Integra: Head-to-Head Comparison

Which DJI digital goggle reigns supreme? We compare the premium Goggles 2 head-to-head with the value-focused Goggles Integra to see which fits your budget.

## Display and Power Differences

DJI has offered two strong digital options in their O3-compatible goggle line. The DJI Goggles 2 features high-fidelity 1080p OLED screens, adjustable diopters, and a touch panel, requiring an external battery. The DJI Goggles Integra, on the other hand, integrates the battery directly into the headband, uses LCOS displays, removes the touch panel for physical buttons, and uses fixed diopter slots (lenses included in the box).

If you require precise diopter adjustments and slightly richer contrast, the Goggles 2 is superior. However, for sheer convenience and lower price, the Integra is a massive winner.

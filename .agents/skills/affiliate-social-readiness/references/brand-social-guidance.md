# FPVLovers Brand and Social Guidance

## Positioning core

Position FPVLovers as a tactical FPV knowledge system, not merely a blog: a practical guide for beginners, builders, racers, long-range and cinematic pilots. Connect field knowledge, product evaluation, safety, tools, and community learning.

Do not overstate AI or RAG in consumer-facing copy. Lead with the pilot outcome; mention the system only where it improves trust or utility.

## Voice

- Cinematic: vivid sense of flight and craft, used sparingly.
- Technical: concrete components, tradeoffs, compatibility, and safety.
- Credible: distinguish measured, sourced, observed, and recommended claims.
- Community-first: teach before selling and invite useful experience, not vanity engagement.
- Calm: avoid `ultimate`, `game-changing`, `must-buy`, guaranteed outcomes, or fake scarcity unless objectively justified.

## Platform roles

| Platform | Primary role | Native shape | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook | discussion, groups, event/community utility | concise context + link or native image | link dumping into groups |
| Instagram | visual identity, carousels, reels | strong first frame, compact caption, useful saveable detail | hashtag walls and generic inspiration |
| YouTube Shorts | searchable micro-education | hook, proof/demo, one takeaway, next step | unsupported product verdicts |
| TikTok | approachable technical discovery | quick myth, mistake, setup, or comparison | trend imitation that weakens credibility |
| X | timely technical notes and threads | sharp observation, data point, linked thread | repetitive promotion |
| Reddit | community contribution and feedback | complete native value, transparent affiliation | disguised promotion, vote requests, affiliate links where disallowed |

## Reusable post anatomy

Use `hook -> useful proof/detail -> tradeoff -> next action -> disclosure when required`.

For sponsor-safe posts, state the relationship near the opening, preserve criticism and safety caveats, and never let approval rights imply editorial control. For deal posts, verify price and availability immediately before publishing, timestamp the observation, and avoid urgency unless the retailer provides a real deadline.

## Brand message constraints

Generate these from verified project truth:

- One-line pitch: outcome + audience + differentiator.
- Affiliate application description: content scope + audience + promotion method + compliance.
- Sponsor pitch: audience relevance + safe formats + editorial independence.
- Social bio: compact pilot benefit, no unverified superlative.
- About section: mission, who it serves, how recommendations work.
- Media-kit intro: partnership value without traffic or reach claims unless supplied.

# Affiliate Readiness Rubric

Score 100 points from evidence verified in the current checkout. Record each deduction and its path or command evidence.

## Categories

| Category | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Trust and legal | 20 | Substantive About, Contact, Privacy, Terms, Editorial Policy, affiliate disclosure, and advertising/sponsorship policy; consistent footer/navigation access; no unsupported partnership claims |
| Commercial content | 20 | Credible mix of reviews, comparisons, buyer guides, budget/starter guides, and evergreen commercial clusters with meaningful depth |
| Editorial quality | 15 | Clear authorship/methodology, pros and cons, evidence-aware claims, corrections/update signals, non-spammy CTAs, and independence language |
| Affiliate compliance | 15 | Accurate program language, conspicuous disclosure near commercial content, sponsored/nofollow link handling, sponsor labeling, and no cloaked or broken CTAs |
| Technical SEO | 15 | Unique metadata, canonical strategy, OG/Twitter coverage, sitemap/robots health, useful structured data, internal links, and no major thin/duplicate commercial pages |
| Social and brand system | 10 | Consistent positioning, platform-specific strategy, reusable templates, brand-safe promotion rules, and sponsor-safe disclosures |
| Measurement and operations | 5 | Honest analytics plan, CTA/event tracking, update cadence, owners, and application evidence checklist without fabricated metrics |

## Rating bands

| Score | Status | Interpretation |
|---:|---|---|
| 90-100 | Ready | Application package is credible; only network-specific live checks remain |
| 75-89 | Conditionally ready | Apply selectively after listed P0 blockers are closed |
| 60-74 | Needs remediation | Trust or commercial-depth gaps are likely to weaken approval |
| 0-59 | Not ready | Do not apply yet; foundational credibility is incomplete |

## Critical blockers

Treat any of these as `Not ready for broad applications` regardless of score:

- claiming participation, approval, testing, traffic, or partnerships without evidence
- missing or inaccessible privacy, contact, disclosure, or terms pages
- affiliate disclosure absent from commercial-content context
- placeholder/broken CTA destinations presented as live purchase links
- copied, thin, unsafe, or materially misleading product advice
- production route or mobile UX failure that blocks core trust/commercial pages

## Scoring rules

1. Count canonical articles, not Markdown/JSON representations.
2. Give no points for a route shell with negligible content.
3. Mark source-only evidence as `implemented, not live-verified`.
4. Mark volatile network rules and product availability as `live verification required`.
5. Award an after score only for changes completed and verified during the run.
6. Keep score improvements proportional. Adding one policy page cannot erase weak commercial depth.
